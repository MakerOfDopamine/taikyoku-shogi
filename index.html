Game 1

Piece Information

Enter a piece name or a piece ID to highlight corresponding pieces on the board. (King is 1000)

Useful Info:
- pressing Alt-V rotates the board.
- holding SHIFT displays alternate moves for certain range-capturing pieces.
- holding X displays alternate moves for lion-like pieces that can move twice in one turn. (You must hold them down through the move.)
- There is no check. Capturing the king wins the game. (There is also no formal win screen)
- The crown prince is not royal and does not promote. (Yes, I know this is not faithful to the original game technically, but programming constraints forced my hand.)
- When pieces promote, black becomes red and white becomes green.
